1. Who We Are

Sassa Solutions is a business based in Marrakech, Morocco that sells ready-to-use digital applications. We operate a WhatsApp-based customer support service to help our customers with questions, purchases, and support.

2. What Information We Collect

When you contact us via WhatsApp, we may collect the following information:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • The content of messages you send us (text, images, documents, voice notes)
  • The date and time of your messages

We do not collect any other personal information unless you voluntarily provide it (such as your name or email address).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely to:

  • Respond to your questions and support requests
  • Improve our customer service
  • Process any orders or inquiries you submit
  • Maintain a record of our conversation for continuity of support
We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. AI-Powered Responses

Our WhatsApp assistant uses artificial intelligence (AI) to understand and respond to your messages automatically. Your messages may be processed by our AI system to generate appropriate responses. This processing is done securely and your data is not used to train AI models.

5. Data Storage

Your conversation data is stored securely in our database. We retain conversation history to provide you with consistent support. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us directly.

6. Data Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously. We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

7. WhatsApp & Meta

Our WhatsApp service operates through the Meta WhatsApp Business API. Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to Meta's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We encourage you to review Meta's privacy policy at whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of our WhatsApp service at any time by simply stopping communication

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
